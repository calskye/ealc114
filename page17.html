This is another photograph from the Gwangju uprising, this time in a colour photograph. Five students carry the body of one of their dead friends. They sport modern clothes that we see people wearing and that we wear in our day to day lives in the present. Imagine what it would feel like to drag the body of your dead friend to safety. Imagine the pain. They look to have a lot of forward momentum. This snapshot freezes them in time, but they could be running. Are they running from bullets? Are they all still in great danger themselves? Do they have more fighting to do? How many of the men in these pictures survive? How old are they? This photograph leaves me with so many questions that hurt to ponder.
