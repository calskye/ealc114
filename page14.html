This photo could be located anywhere in the United States. This common landscape becomes almost dystopian in the repeatability of the restaraunt chains the same roads, same signs, same cars- just in a new location. America is designed around a sprawling network of highway systems that give rises to these copy-paste strip malls. I'm sure everyone viewing this picture could name this street. Although they might all be different places, they will be nearly identical. Take a moment to draw yourself into what America means to you personally, what it has given you, what it has taken from you. Think about your own community and travels through the United States and beyond.
