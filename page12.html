We now take a leap through space and time into Jim Crow era southern United States. Everyone had technically had the right to vote, but the government was determined to supress the voices of Black voters by instating racially charged poll taxes that barred many poorer individuals from being able to register and vote. This is another horrific case of governments actively harming and supressing their people. America, colloquially referred to the "Land of the Free", was not always truly free for everyone. Even today, as we will see in reference to police brutality and protestor suppression and violence, there is a multitude of work that remains to be done.
