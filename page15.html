China's One Child Policy has left the country with a massive problem for the future. The population is ageing, and people do not seem to want to have kids, leading to massively shrinking populations forcasts. In 2016 the Two Child Policy came in to effect to encourage more births, but the population kept dropping. Now young people in China are avoiding marriage and starting a family in order for more freedom to focus on their careers. This leads to a shrinking labor force, excessive strain on the healthcare system, and economic slump. The working age populations will be tasked with caring for the elderly, with one only child caring for four grand parents and two aging parents. This is the byproduct of a harmful government policy gone wrong.
