This is another photo of what Gwangju looks like today. The blue sky, rolling green hills, colourful apartment high rises, businesses, and office buildings. The city's skyline seems so vibrant and alive. It is easy to imagine that the horror of the 1970s has faded into oblivion. However, the same streets that were once littered with brutilized bodies are now filled with commuters who lived through this time and remember those killed. The once fervent student protestors that had survived the ordeal now might've settled down into older age and more conservative views, left with a void of who they once were during the uprising.
