The West has seen a surge of craze for Korean popular culture. This image is taken from the television show Squid Game that was produced in Korea and gained international following. I remember there was a time when I could not talk with anyone and not hear Squid Game mentioned at least once. In addition to Korean television and moviews, Kpop, Kbeauty, Korean food, has all reached the mainstream in media consumption of Western societies. Perhaps it is how all of these things create a very vivid fantasy land for their viewers. Either we are lost in a dystopian world, or entertained with the seemingly perfect one of Kpop where everyone is young, beautiful, and talented. For many people, Squid Game and Kpop is what Korea means to them, the cultural context and historical tumult that built Korea into the cultural powerhouse it is today mostly goes unnoticed.
