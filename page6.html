This photo, taken from the archives depicts student protestors leaning out of a bus calling out slogans during a demonstration. Their energy and vitality crackles on the page, making this image particularly chilling once we are made aware of the brutality that these students faced in order to be part of something bigger to establish Korea’s democracy. As a young student, my heart is pained as I see both my peers and myself in this image of students who would most likely go on to suffer horrific brutality inflicted by their own government designed to protect them.
