Police brutality has been a central problem throughout the history of the United States. It refers to the excessive and unlawful use of force by law enforcement. America has a justice system where verdicts are delivered through trials. One individual does not reserve the right to kill someone on the scene of the crime in most cases. Both police brutality in the United States and the events of the Gwangju Uprising raise questions about the use of excessive force agaisnt civilians and how that impacts public trust and democratic accountability. In 2020 massive protests were mobilized in response to the killing of George Floyd. This body cam footage draws the viewers into a world that they hope they never have to be faced with and transforms us into the antagoist.
