This image was taken in downtown Gwangju during the uprising in May. It highlights the massive scale of the protests, but also calls us to imagine what was to follow. It is likely that most of the people in this image were to be slaughtered by their own military at the command of their own leader. Recalling these points makes the image showing Chun Doo-hwan's arrest even more heavy. This photo shows so much energy and chaos, just like the following image of the protesters leaning from out from the bus, most of those in the crowd were young students, their lives brutally cut short and for those who survived, stamped with the mark of trauma and violence forever.
