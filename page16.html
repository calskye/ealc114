I read both of Nobel winning author Han Kang's books surrounding trauma. The first book, Human Acts, follows different characters involved in the uprising. Their lives, deaths, and futures for those who survived. Her second book focuses on trauma surrounding the Jeju Uprising. It is called We Do not Part, telling an intense story of loss and friendship marred by ghosts of the past. These two books were meant to be read in companion with eachother. Han Kang herself was born in Gwangju but her family moved to Seoul just months before the 1980 Gwangju Uprising, leaving her with a void in understanding exactly what had gone on during that time. She grew up surrounded by whispers about the trauma, loss of human dignity, and scars that the uprising gauged into Gwangju. The books are meant for Kang herself to uncover and make sense of this history as much as they are meant for the world to better understand.
