This photo is also taken from Gwangju. Gwangju held its first Queer Culture Festival, featuring a pride parade, in 2018. Korea is a very conservative society. Many individuals practice conservative Christianity that discourages homosexuality. In this scene people take to the streets, not in protest, but in celebration of pride and the society that they want to feel free living in. These celebrations took place along the same streets that students had died on during the May movement. They are pushing for recognition, visibility, protection, and equality.
