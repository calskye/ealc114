The students involved in the uprising knew that they were part of something bigger than themselves. Their sacrafices lead to the establishment of a democracy in South Korea. This image is particulary haunting because they are parading the flag of their country through the same streets where they would be killed by their own military. In the novel Human Acts, author Han Kang, also pictured in this gallery, details the confusion and pain that was felt by family members and mourners when they would come to retrieve their dead relatives in coffins wrapped in this flag. The student protesters felt so much energy to stick up for the vision of their country that they loved, only to be brutilized.
