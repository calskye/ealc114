Yet another case of violence of the government against its people– China’s One Child Policy. I had heard of this policy, and was notably reminded of its existence when it was revoked in recent history, but I had never before considered the violence that enforcing such a policy would require. Now this violence was targeted specifically towards women. Women who were pregnant, but already had a child were removed from their homes, packed in animal cages, their babies forcefully aborted, then sterilized. Their autonomy was completely destroyed with this gender based violence. These photos exude death and violence and are filled with spectres of the past. Questions unanswered, questions that feel too difficult to engage with and better left ignored. Our reading was right, the war never really ended. Even today, in supposedly the freest nation, the United States, protestors are slaughtered for being active against oppression. George Orwell’s quote: “If you want a picture of the future, imagine a boot stamping on a human face—forever. ” rings truer than ever.
